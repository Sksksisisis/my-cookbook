Banana Oat Chocolate Chip Cookies

Simple cookies made with bananas, oats, and chocolate chips. Check your class recipe sheet for exact quantities.

Ingredients

  • Bananas — use the amount specified by your class recipe
  • Oats — use the amount specified by your class recipe
  • Chocolate chips — ⅓ cup for the cookie mixture, plus extra for the drizzle
  • Cooking oil spray
  • Icing sugar, optional

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (about 177°C).
  2. Line a rimmed baking sheet with parchment paper and lightly spray it with cooking oil.
  3. Mash the bananas in a bowl until mostly smooth.
  4. Add the oats and stir until combined.
  5. Mix in ⅓ cup of chocolate chips.
  6. Use two spoons to portion the mixture onto the baking sheet.
  7. Bake for 12–15 minutes, checking for doneness.
  8. Carefully melt extra chocolate chips in the microwave in short bursts, stirring between each.
  9. Let the cookies cool for at least 10 minutes.
  10. Finish with chocolate drizzle or a light sprinkle of icing sugar.
  11. Clean the kitchen and return dishes to their proper cupboards.
