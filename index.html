Week 1 · Baking lab

Banana Oat Chocolate Chip Bake

This is a general outline based on the bake shown in my food lab photo. For exact ingredient quantities, follow the original class recipe.

Ingredients

  • Bananas, using the quantity from the class recipe
  • Oats, using the quantity from the class recipe
  • Chocolate chips, as directed by the class recipe
  • Cooking spray or parchment paper, if required
  • Extra chocolate and banana slices for decoration

Preparation

  1. Gather the ingredients and prepare the workspace.
  2. Mash the bananas in a mixing bowl.
  3. Add the oats and mix until combined.
  4. Stir in the chocolate chips.
  5. Portion the mixture onto a prepared baking tray.
  6. Bake using the temperature and timing in the class recipe.
  7. Allow the bake to cool before decorating.
  8. Add chocolate drizzle and banana slices.
  9. Present the finished dish and clean the workspace.

Reflection: My finished dish featured chocolate drizzle and banana slices. I used presentation techniques to make the final result look appealing.
